Server Rules

Short version: be kind, build your own stuff, and play fair. Breaking the rules can get you warned, muted, jailed or banned depending on how serious it is.

Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats or discrimination of any kind, in game or on Discord.

No griefing or stealing

Don't break, alter or take from anything you didn't build, even if it is unclaimed. Claim your builds with a golden shovel to protect them.

No cheating

Hacked clients, x-ray (mods, texture packs or exploits), auto-clickers, duplication glitches and other unfair advantages are banned. Allowed: OptiFine/Sodium, minimaps without entity radar, and shaders.

No lag machines

Don't build anything designed to lag the server. Keep large farms reasonable and add an on/off switch to redstone clocks.

PvP only with consent

Only fight players who agreed to it. Trapping, spawn-killing and combat-logging to avoid a fight are not allowed.

Keep chat clean

No spam, excessive caps, advertising other servers, or sharing personal information (yours or anyone else's).

Appropriate builds and names

No offensive builds, skins, signs, book text or item names.

Report bugs, don't abuse them

Found an exploit? Report it to staff privately. Abusing it gets you banned, reporting it gets you a reward.

Listen to staff

Staff decisions are final in the moment. If you disagree, open a ticket on Discord afterwards and we'll review it calmly.

One account per person

Alt accounts need staff approval. Ban evasion results in a permanent ban on all accounts.

Need help or want to report someone? Open a ticket in our Discord with screenshots if you can. In game, all block changes are logged, so stolen items and griefs can be rolled back.
