What makes us different

Pure survival at its core, with a few quality-of-life upgrades that reward you for playing.

⛏️

Skill levels

Earn XP from mining, farming, fighting and fishing. Level up to unlock extra hearts, Speed and Haste.

🛡️

Protected land

Claim your base with /claim or a golden shovel. Nobody can grief or steal from inside your claim, and every block change is logged.

💼

Fair economy

Trade with players, set homes and warp around. No pay-to-win: the store sells cosmetics and perks only.

💬

Active community

In-game chat is linked to Discord, so you can talk to players even when you're not online.
