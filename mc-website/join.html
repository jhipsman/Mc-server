How to Join

New to the server? Follow these steps and you'll be in game within a couple of minutes.

Get Minecraft: Java Edition

You need an official copy of Minecraft: Java Edition for PC, Mac or Linux. Our version: 1.21.x.

Bedrock Edition (consoles, phones, Windows "Bedrock") can't join.

Join our Discord and request whitelist

The server is whitelisted to keep griefers out. Join our Discord, then type:

/whitelist username:YourMinecraftName

A staff member will review it, usually within a few hours. The bot will DM you when you're approved.

Add the server

In the Minecraft Launcher, pick the version shown in step 1 and press Play. Then click Multiplayer → Add Server and paste the address.

Join and protect your stuff

Double-click the server to join. When you've found a spot for your base, claim it:

Stand in the middle of your base and type /claim, or hold a golden shovel and right-click two opposite corners of your land. Type /sethome to save your base so you can /home back to it.

Read the rules and have fun

Take a minute to read the rules. Then start mining, farming and fighting to level up. Check your progress any time with /stats.

Can't connect? Make sure your launcher version matches the server version, you were approved for the whitelist, and you typed the address exactly. Still stuck? Ask in our Discord.
